The Genesis of FACE WASH

Where Relaxation Meets
Uncompromised Care

Founded in the prime location of Dhanmondi at Momtaz Plaza (Ground Floor), FACE WASH was established with a singular vision: to offer Dhaka's women a parlour experience that feels genuinely restorative, hygienic, and personalized.

Too often, women in Dhaka experience rushed salon appointments, overcrowded spaces, and questionable tool cleanliness. At FACE WASH, we broke that mold. Our studio is built with spacious private care cubicles, soothing ambient lighting, UV sterilization, and female practitioners who consult with you before touching a strand of hair or applying a drop of serum.

The 4 Pillars of FACE WASH

Sterilized & Clinical

All metal shears, tweezers, and extractors undergo hospital-grade UV-C sterilization boxes. Towels and capes are freshly laundered and sealed for each guest.

All-Female Specialists

Our staff consists of warm, highly trained female stylists, estheticians, and therapists dedicated to your comfort and personal styling goals.

Private Care Suites

We provide private, quiet suites designed specifically for hijab-wearing women, intimate body spa sessions, and bridal draping.

100% Genuine Products

We never compromise on product authenticity. We exclusively use L'Oréal Professionnel, Moroccanoil, Olaplex, Dermalogica, and certified organic oils.
